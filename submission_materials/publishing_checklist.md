# Recall Publishing Checklist

## Required per teammate

1. Publish `technical_article.md` as one public, linkable article.
2. Publish `linkedin_post.md` on LinkedIn after replacing `[PASTE_PUBLIC_ARTICLE_URL]`.
3. Share the article as a Reddit Link post using `reddit_post.md`.

## Required per team

1. Record one public 2–5 minute demo video.
2. Show the problem, the Recall workflow, and Hindsight recall/retention.
3. Publish the video on YouTube with a suitable thumbnail.

## Recommended article platforms

- Medium
- Dev.to
- Hashnode
- Substack
- LinkedIn Articles

Choose one platform for the article. Do not publish the same article everywhere unless you want additional reach.

## Important content rules

- Keep the article and LinkedIn post focused on the project, not the event.
- Do not mention the hackathon in the article, social post, title, hashtags, or Reddit description.
- Add the public GitHub repository link to the LinkedIn post.
- Add the Hindsight repository as a comment on the LinkedIn post:
  https://github.com/vectorize-io/hindsight
- Tag Code.in on LinkedIn and in the article if required by the organizer.
- Use synthetic screenshots and never show `.env`, API keys, databases, or private customer data.
